CCTV SETUP, BARANGAY MANTULANG APP v5
=====================================

WHAT THE APP CAN SHOW
A web browser cannot play RTSP (rtsp://) directly, which is what most IP cameras, DVRs and NVRs speak.
So the app plays links that browsers understand:
  1. HLS  (.m3u8)       best choice for live video
  2. MJPEG stream       simple, works with many cheap cameras
  3. Snapshot image     a JPEG link that the app re-loads every few seconds (lowest bandwidth)
  4. Embed              YouTube Live or any web player page
  5. Video link         mp4 / webm
All links MUST start with https:// (the app is https, so browsers block http links).

EASIEST WAY TO CONNECT A NORMAL IP CAMERA / NVR (free software)
  1. Put a small always-on computer (old PC, mini PC, Raspberry Pi) at the barangay hall, on the same network as the cameras.
  2. Install go2rtc (or MediaMTX). Give it each camera's RTSP address. It turns RTSP into HLS.
       go2rtc HLS link:     http://<computer>:1984/api/stream.m3u8?src=<camera-name>
       MediaMTX HLS link:   http://<computer>:8888/<camera-name>/index.m3u8
  3. Make it reachable over https. Free option: Cloudflare Tunnel (cloudflared). It gives you an https address
     that points to that computer, without opening router ports. A tunnel on your own domain gives a permanent link.
  4. Allow your website to read the stream (CORS):
       go2rtc:    in go2rtc.yaml:   api: { origin: "*" }
       MediaMTX:  in mediamtx.yml:  hlsAllowOrigin: '*'    (better: put your site URL, no trailing slash)
  5. In the app: CCTV > Add camera > type "Live stream (HLS)" > paste the https link > Save.

SIMPLEST TEST (no computer needed)
  - Choose "Embed" and paste a YouTube Live link, or choose "Snapshot" and paste a camera's JPEG snapshot https link.
  - NOTE: an "unlisted" YouTube link is NOT private. Use it only for cameras you are happy to make public.

SECURITY RULES OF THUMB
  - NEVER put the camera username/password in the link. The app refuses links that contain them.
  - Links are stored in Firestore. Officials can read every link; residents only read cameras marked Public.
    So treat every link as a secret among officials. Use hard-to-guess camera names on the gateway.
  - Never port-forward a camera or DVR straight to the internet. Change all default camera passwords.
  - Keep "Officials only" as the default. Use "Public" only for open areas (plaza, road, flood marker).
  - Every official view and snapshot is written to the Access log (CCTV > Access log). Keep officials to a minimum.
  - "Monitor mode" keeps the screen awake and disables auto sign-out. Use it only on a locked-down office device.

PRIVACY AND LEGAL (RA 10173)
  - Get written approval from the Punong Barangay and name who may watch and for what purpose.
  - Put visible CCTV signs where cameras are. Cover public areas only, never private homes, toilets or inside offices.
  - Decide how long recordings are kept and who may export them. Follow the National Privacy Commission's CCTV guidance.
  - This app shows LIVE views and saves snapshots on the device that clicked "snapshot". It does not record video.
    For recording, use your NVR or go2rtc/MediaMTX recording on the barangay hall computer.
  - A camera view is not a guarantee that someone is watching. Always publish 911 and hotline numbers.
